Scope — what this map covers and what it leaves out on purpose

In scope

  • Synthetic oligonucleotide actives: antisense (incl. gapmers and splice-switching), siRNA, aptamers; conjugated (GalNAc, antibody, peptide) and lipid-nanoparticle-formulated forms.
  • Jurisdictions: ICH, EU centralised procedure and Clinical Trials Regulation, US FDA and federal statute, Japan MHLW/PMDA oligonucleotide texts, UK trial authorisation.
  • Development from candidate selection to post-approval obligations arising from paediatric and conditional-approval commitments.
  • Non-binding consensus (OSWG, Toxicologic Pathology Forum, review articles) where regulators are known to rely on it and no binding text exists — marked as such.

Out of scope (deliberately)

  • mRNA and gene-editing products (own regulatory frameworks, ATMP in the EU), except where a document explicitly covers both.
  • China NMPA, Health Canada, Australia TGA, Swissmedic and other national frameworks.
  • Pharmacovigilance, risk-management plans, labelling, pricing and reimbursement, GMP inspection law, environmental risk assessment.
  • Product-specific precedent (assessment reports, labels) — a possible future node type.

Reading the verification fields

  • Source opened: the official page or PDF was read during the check.
  • URL on official site: the document was located on the issuing body’s domain but not opened.
  • Secondary source opened: a non-official source (law-firm summary, library record) was read because the primary text could not be reached.
  • Not checked live: carried from prior knowledge. As of 28 October 2026 no document is in this state.
  • Negative results are recorded with the date they were established, so that “nothing found” is distinguishable from “not looked”.

Using this page

Filters and presets

  • Filters apply to all views. Dim keeps non-matching documents at low opacity; Hide removes them, turns a programme-type selection into a filter, and closes up empty rows and lanes.
  • One built-in preset per programme type (jurisdictions, gate range, programme type, Hide). Save as preset stores the current filters for the selected programme type in this browser only; Built-in presets forgets saved ones.
  • The active view is in the address bar (#map, #explore, #register, #notes), so a view can be linked.

Export and print

  • Copy SVG copies Figure 1 in the current theme and filter state; Copy CSV copies the register rows currently shown with all data fields; Copy JSON copies the whole graph.
  • Print renders Figure 1 on A4 landscape pages (split at lane boundaries), then Panel 1 and the filtered Table 1 in portrait.
  • The data files (JSON, CSV, schema) and the generator are in the accompanying package; data are CC BY 4.0, code MIT.

Keyboard and colour

  • Tab moves between bars in Figure 1, ←/→ between keys of a series bar, Enter opens the detail sheet, Esc closes it; ←/→ also move between the view tabs.
  • No status is carried by colour alone: in force = solid outline; draft = short dashes; pending = long dashes; programme = rounded; non-binding consensus = dotted; unverified = hatched fill and red key; superseded = struck through; mixed series = dash-dot. Jurisdiction is always also given as a text tag.
